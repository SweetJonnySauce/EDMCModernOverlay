## Decisions and invariants to preserve

- Follow `AGENTS.md` and the approved detailed design before implementation.
- Preserve the `fix219` backend boundary: generic consumers must not import compositor-private
  behavior or dispatch from raw backend/helper/presenter identities.
- Keep presentation and input as separate behavioral interfaces with independent revisions;
  do not reintroduce a shared ambiguous `snapshot()` method.
- One runtime owns one immutable identity and stable service instances for its process lifetime.
- Start is attempted once; stop is bounded, reverse-order, idempotent, terminal, and safe after
  partial start.
- Keep unavailable/unimplemented presentation fail-closed and never route an unimplemented
  Wayland environment through a nominal shared fallback.
- Keep support policy, reviewed evidence, and live health independent.
- Use Step 01's schema-v1 codec and privacy boundaries. Keep failures/history bounded and redact
  tokens, owner IDs, handles, titles, commands, exceptions, and personal paths before
  serialization.
- Keep the paper backend and reusable factory implementation under test support and out of
  production selection/imports.
- Continue lift-then-prove increments; do not rewrite Phase 19 or broaden KDE/KWin, capture,
  geometry, content, settings, portal, or Windows scope.
- Use the root `.venv` for development/tests. Keep `overlay_client/.venv` runtime-only.
